Design Services Agreement

Between Harbor & Pine Studio and Bluefin Outdoor Co.

1. Scope

The Studio will deliver the Spring 2027 catalogue: art direction, layout of up to 64 pages, retouching and print management, as described in the attached brief.

2. Timeline

Work starts on 10 November 2026. Final files are delivered by 15 January 2027, with two rounds of review on each milestone.

3. Fees and payment

Fees follow the agreed estimate. Invoices are payable within 30 days. Extra rounds of changes are billed at the day rate.

4. Ownership

On full payment, the Client owns the final artwork. The Studio may show the work in its portfolio after publication.
